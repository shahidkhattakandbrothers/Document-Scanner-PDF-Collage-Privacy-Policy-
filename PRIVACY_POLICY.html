This Privacy Policy describes how Document Scanner PDF Collage ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Shahid Khattak, handles your information when you use our mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users.

1. Information Collection and Usage

A. Camera Access (CAMERA Permission)

  • Purpose: The App requests access to your device's camera to allow you to capture physical documents, books, IDs, photos, and images directly within the App.
  • Data Handling: Camera data is processed entirely on your device in real-time for scanning, cropping, frame adjustment, filtering, and collage creation. Images captured using the camera are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to external servers or shared with third parties without your explicit action.

B. Storage and Media Access

  • Purpose: The App requests permission to access device storage / media library to:
    1. Import existing photos or document images from your gallery into the App for editing, document scanning, or collage creation.
    2. Save generated PDFs, collages, and scanned images locally on your device storage.
  • Data Handling: The App only reads images you explicitly select from your gallery and writes output files (scanned documents, collages, and PDFs) to your device's local storage. Your files remain under your complete control on your local device.

C. On-Device Local Data Storage

  • Local Database: The App uses an on-device database (SQLite) to organize and index your saved document titles, categories (e.g., All Documents, Book Mode, Collage Mode), timestamps, and local file paths.
  • No Cloud Uploads: We do not operate remote servers to store, backup, or index your scanned documents, collages, or personal files. All document processing happens locally on your Android device.

2. Document Sharing

When you choose to share a document, image, or collage using the App's built-in share feature:

  • The App uses standard Android system file sharing (FileProvider) to transfer the selected file directly to the external app you choose (such as email clients, messaging apps, or cloud drives).
  • We do not intercept, track, or monitor shared content.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The App does not require account creation, registration, or sign-in. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App is safe for general audiences and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Since all data remains locally on the user's device, no personal information is harvested or transmitted.

5. Security

We prioritize the security of your data. Because all scanning, photo editing, collage creation, and file management functions are performed locally on your device, your documents and photos are secured by your Android device's built-in security features and storage sandboxing.

6. Your Data Rights & Deletion

Since all data (documents, collages, scanned images, and metadata) is stored locally on your device:

  • You can delete any individual document, photo, or collage directly within the App at any time.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data via Android Settings will permanently remove all locally stored database entries and app-specific files.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates.
